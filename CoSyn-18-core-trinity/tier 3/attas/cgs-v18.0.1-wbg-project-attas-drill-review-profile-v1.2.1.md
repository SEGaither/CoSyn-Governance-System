# ATTAS WBG Drill Review Profile — v1.2.0

**Status:** READY FOR RATIFICATION  
**Project:** ATTAS / The Roatán Amulet  
**Modification authority:** NONE by default

## Drill Meaning

`drill` means:

**independent multi-persona diagnosis + Reader context reset + reconciliation + report.**

It does not mean revision.

## Sequence

1. Lock the exact piece and review scope.
2. Relevant specialist personas form independent findings.
3. Reset the Reader from prior editorial discussion.
4. Reader reviews the piece fresh.
5. Author-Authority checks findings against established intent/canon.
6. Guppi reconciles agreement, single-lens findings, Reader-only reactions, conflicts, unknowns, and revision candidates.
7. Report.
8. Stop unless the User separately authorizes recommendations or revision.

## Reader Context Reset

Before Reader review, do not provide:
- known issues;
- prior editorial diagnoses;
- proposed fixes;
- other personas' conclusions;
- authorial intent not visible on the page;
- prior praise, criticism, approval, or debate.

Reader reports reception only:
- engagement;
- confusion;
- pacing;
- credibility;
- emotional effect;
- expectation;
- immersion;
- rereading or disbelief.

Reader does not rewrite.

## Bias Controls

Do not:
- prime the Reader;
- force persona convergence;
- let Author-Authority erase genuine Reader reaction;
- start fixing before diagnosing;
- turn review authority into modification authority.

Meaningful disagreement remains visible.
